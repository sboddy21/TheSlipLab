Build around NBA on most weekdays and NFL around its main game windows. Use another sport when a verified Lab signal is clearly stronger.

Keep one original per posting day and log three useful replies in active NBA or NFL threads. Record impressions and profile visits for the replies as well as the originals. ## The daily loop (about 25–35 minutes, Eastern time)

- **9:30 a.m. review:** Check the last seven days, capture 24-hour numbers for the latest eligible post(s), and identify the weakest step: reach, conversation, profile conversion, or site conversion. If private numbers are unavailable, supply a screenshot or a short aggregate snapshot. Keep incomplete metrics blank. Review alerts and fixtures against official sources, then select one Lab signal worth sharing.
- **60–120 minutes before the game:** Publish one original insight tied to a verified player, role, matchup, or market move. Lead with why it matters. Show one piece of evidence and give readers a simple choice or ask one specific question when it fits. If the inputs are stale or unverified, use an explainer or completed receipt instead.
- **Around the post:** Spend 10–15 minutes writing 3–5 useful replies under relevant team reporters, analysts, and smaller sports creators already discussing the subject. Respond to every substantive reply on the Lab’s post with context or a follow-up. Use personal, topic-specific replies without dropping links into other people’s conversations.
- **After the result:** Save what was said before the game and the actual outcome. Publish a receipt where it adds something, including misses. Keep the next morning’s log up to date. Each Sunday, pick one thing to repeat, one to change, and one to drop.

## Drafts you can adapt today

- **NBA matchup read:** “The first number I’m checking before [game]: [Player]’s minutes. The Lab’s read changed from [X] to [Y] after [verified update]. Which matters more here: the extra minutes or the tougher matchup?” Attach one current, timestamped board crop.
- **NFL role update:** “[Player]’s box score looked quiet. The usage told a different story: [verified snap or route change]. If that role holds, here’s the matchup I’m watching next: [one reason].”
- **The Receipt:** “Before kickoff we expected [signal] because [reason]. Final: [result]. We got [part] right and missed [part]. Here’s the input I’ll check earlier next time: [input].”
- **Conversion post:** “Here’s how I go from a crowded slate to one matchup worth watching: [two quick steps]. Try it with a free Slip Lab account: [tagged link].” Only name capabilities verified in the free flow.
- **Useful reply:** “The part I’d add is [specific confirmed data point]. It matters here because [one-sentence implication].” Tailor it to the conversation.

## Measurement and decisions

Use the [X Post Activity Dashboard](https://business.x.com/help/tweet-activity-dashboard) if your account has access. Track public views separately from dashboard impressions. X’s engagement total includes several kinds of clicks, so track audience replies and reposts separately; omit your own replies from that count. Read public metrics in context: views are not unique people.

| Signal | What to log and what to change |
| --- | --- |
| Reach | Median impressions per original at 24 hours. If reach is flat after 10–12 comparable posts, change the distribution window or join conversations around a narrower sport/topic. |
| Interaction | Audience replies, reposts and likes per post; main rate = (audience replies + reposts) ÷ impressions. If reach rises without conversation, simplify the hook and ask a specific question people can answer. |
| Followers | Daily follower total, weekly net change, profile clicks and post-attributed follows if available. If posts prompt profile clicks but follower growth stays flat, test the bio and pin promise. |
| Traffic | X link clicks and tagged site sessions by link placement. If followers or profile visits rise yet sessions stay flat, put a clearer benefit and one visible free-account CTA on the pinned post. |
| Sign-ups | Completed, preferably verified, free accounts attributed to tagged visits; compare sign-ups ÷ tagged sessions. If there are at least 20 tracked visits without sign-ups, inspect mobile load, sign-up errors and email confirmation before changing the content. |
