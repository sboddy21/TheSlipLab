What Lab Notes are built to do.

The live boards tell you what the current data says. Lab Notes explain how to interpret it. These articles focus on repeatable process, transparent limitations and the difference between identifying a favorable profile and predicting a certain outcome. Rankings and model signals are research tools—not guarantees or sportsbook odds.
